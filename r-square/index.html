1. What is AI?

AI = Computers learning from data to solve problems

  • Like a student learning from notes & practice
  • Examples: Google Translate, selfie filters, YouTube recommendations

2. Why is AI Growing Fast?

  • More data from phones, apps, games, and sports trackers
  • Better computers — faster and cheaper
  • Smarter programs — deep learning, neural networks

  • Personalised learning apps that match your pace

4. AI in Games

  • Game testing bots for better quality

5. AI in Sports

  • AI-based coaching tips

  • Fan engagement through AI apps

6. AI in Healthcare

  • Personalised treatment suggestions

  • Smart health tracking devices
